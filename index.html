The Peens Are Coming

The Champeens want three seats upstairs. They have a horse, a baby, and a list of demands. The list was written inside the palace.

They came up the red carpet on a Thursday: a man on a horse, a man in a poncho waving trade offers, a man in a blue wig, and a baby. The doorman did not know where to look.

They wanted seats in the Premier. They started at four. Boom heard the number and threw up twice, so the army came down to three and called it mercy.

Then a window opened on the top floor. The Guy Next Door leaned out. He owns a team up there. It is 2‑7, it is named Shineys Smacker this week, and he is not taking questions about that. He looked at the horse for a long time. Then he wrote the army its demands and dropped them out the window.
